Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses, timings, and learning process.

I can understand English, but I hesitate to speak. Will this course help me?

Yes! In fact, 90% of our students face this exact issue. Understanding is passive, whereas speaking requires active neurological speech habit. Our curriculum focuses entirely on spoken drills, quick speech reflexes, and eliminating the mental Hindi-to-English translation loop.

Are classes conducted online or offline?

We provide interactive live online batches via Google Meet / Zoom with high-engagement smart classrooms, as well as offline weekend workshops and 1-on-1 mentorship. You can join comfortably from anywhere across India or abroad.

What is the duration and batch size?

Our comprehensive batches typically run for 2 to 3 months with both weekday (morning & evening) and weekend options for college students and working professionals. Batch sizes are strictly kept small (10-12 students) to ensure every student speaks daily.

How do I book a Free Demo session?

Simply click on any "Book Free Demo" button or the floating WhatsApp button on this page. Our team will instantly connect with you on WhatsApp to allocate your demo slot and share free study materials.

Will I receive course material and certificates?

Yes, every student receives digital cheat sheets, daily vocabulary PDFs, interview frameworks, audio listening files, and a recognized Certificate of Completion upon course graduation.
